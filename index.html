About Us

History of Western University

Western University was established on November 17, 2003, under Sub-decree No. 72 by its founder, Mr. Te Laurent. That same year, Mr. Laurent also inaugurated the Western International School. Following approval from the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport, the university expanded its operations in 2005 by establishing a branch in Kampong Cham province. The University is led by Rector Te Laurent, a distinguished alumnus of the University of Sorbonne in Paris, France, where he earned a degree in Economic Science, specializing in Finance and Banking.

Committed to excellence, Western University is dedicated to strengthening its administrative framework and academic curriculum. The institution has embarked on a strategic initiative to align its teaching quality with international standards, thereby contributing significantly to the development of Cambodia’s human capital.
